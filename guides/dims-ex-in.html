A-M-T Command Guide · 09

DIMS EX&IN

Interior and exterior dimensions for plans, sections and elevations, including linked architectural and structural models. Choose one view or several, and every dimension is checked after it is placed.

Plans · Sections · ElevationsSeveral views at onceLinked models

How the command works

Open DIMS EX&IN from the A-M-T ribbon, tick the views to dimension, choose DIM INTERIOR, DIM EXTERIOR or both, and click Run. The views run one after another; a summary appears at the end and the full log is saved on the Desktop and copied to the clipboard.

01 · Choose the viewsSearch the list of plans, sections and elevations, or press Current view to keep only the open view.
02 · Choose what to dimensionTick DIM INTERIOR and/or DIM EXTERIOR; each one shows its own settings under it.
03 · Run & reviewRead the summary per view and check the dimensions in Revit. Running again replaces the A-M-T dimensions of that view.

The DIMS EX&IN window

01 · Views to dimension

The list shows every plan, section and elevation of the file. The open view is ticked when the window opens. Current view keeps only the open view; Clear removes every tick.

02 · DIM INTERIOR

Dimension distance from the wall sets how far the chain is placed from the wall; the overall goes the same distance beyond it. Split a side separates a room side when its wall pieces step more than the given distance. Include door and window widths adds openings to the chains. The list of linked models lets you leave out a link.

03 · DIM EXTERIOR

Set the distance of the first line from the building and the gap between the lines.

Uses the last dimension type used in Revit.

What it creates

Plans · interior

One continuous chain per room side with every break, column, door and window, plus the overall. Room outlines are read from the real room shape, so chains reach the actual room edge; flush columns are not measured.

Plans · shafts

Length and width of every shaft from inside, measured once even when it is drawn in both architectural and structural models. Rooms drawn inside shafts or stair openings are skipped.

Plans · exterior

Three lines per facade: facade detail, grids and overall, including curved walls and curtain walls.

Sections

Exterior: edge detail, levels and overall on both sides. Interior: finish, door or window, ceiling and slab soffit for every floor on the line with most doors; parapets measured from the structural slab top.

Elevations

Exterior lines on both sides, and one detail line plus one overall about every 5 m along the facade, from slab faces, sills, heads and curtain grid lines.

Checked dimensions

Every dimension is compared with the expected distance after it is placed. A wrong reference is removed and the chain is tried again; anything skipped is listed in the log.

Before you run

  • Make sure the views show the rooms, walls and links you want dimensioned.
  • Place one dimension by hand first if you want a specific dimension type: the command uses the last type used.
  • Run on one view first, check the result, then run on the rest.
  • If something looks wrong, send the log from the Desktop (A-M-T_Dims_log.txt).
